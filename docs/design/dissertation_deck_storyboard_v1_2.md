## Source and interpretation controls
- Original uploaded `GQD_presentation.pptx` provided the title and research themes. Later placeholder slides have been reauthored from actual scientific scope/status rather than retained as placeholder prose.
- Claims reflect the canonical `SCIENTIFIC_STATUS.md` **controlled 2026-11-13**, `docs/scientific_scope.md`, and `docs/acceptance_gates_v2.md`.
- Slide 19's corrected estimates are **raw SMD binding energies plus gas-phase BSSE corrections**. They are not a fully solvated counterpoise calculation.
- Slide 20's M06-2X numbers are electronic single-point binding energies on B3LYP optimized geometries; they are neither M06-2X Gibbs free energies nor BSSE-corrected values.
- Phase II and Phase III remain **planned** in this slide edition.
- Stylized geometry illustrations and branding motifs are **conceptual**, not Gaussian coordinate renderings or validated computed molecular shapes.
